# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors considering plants from Underwood nursery: people browsing catalog items, reading care content, checking nursery credibility, and looking for contact details before calling or visiting.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Underwood as a trustworthy plant nursery, helps visitors discover available plants and useful care information, and makes contact or visit decisions simple.

## Brand Personality

Natural, calm, knowledgeable. The interface should feel grounded and helpful, with enough warmth to fit a nursery without becoming decorative or fussy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic template landing-page patterns, over-decorated botanical styling, low-contrast muted text, and motion that distracts from practical information.

## Design Principles

Show useful details at the moment they matter.
Keep visual changes aligned with the existing Underwood palette and component rhythm.
Prefer calm confidence over ornamental flourishes.
Make practical next steps easy to scan on mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, semantic labels, keyboard-safe interactions, and reduced-motion alternatives for animation.
